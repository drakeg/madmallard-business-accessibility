# Scanning and Reports

1. Sign in as an administrator.
2. Enter an HTTP(S) site URL that you own or have explicit permission to assess. Confirm authorization in the scan form.
3. Start a scan. Local and hosted capabilities differ: hosted Vercel disables optional Lighthouse until validated; the normal scan includes axe accessibility, on-page SEO, and HTML-Validate diagnostics.
4. Open the audit in history to inspect exact findings, affected elements, review notes, and remediation guidance.
5. Download the printable HTML report or JSON evidence as needed, and rescan the same URL to compare before and after changes.

Customer accounts can view, download, and compare only scans assigned to them. They cannot start scans or alter findings. Scan ownership changes require an administrator. Do not distribute customer content through public issue or discussion posts.

A clean automated report is **not** certification of accessibility, compliance, or search ranking. Keyboard navigation, zoom, assistive technology, real human testing, and qualified review still matter.
